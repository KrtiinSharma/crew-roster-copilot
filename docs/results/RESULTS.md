Generated by `uv run crew-eval report` from the JSON files in this folder. Scenarios and solver results are seeded and reproducible; timings vary by machine.

## Optimiser benchmark

50 seeded disruption scenarios (seed 11, ruleset `dgca_current`, CP-SAT deterministic time limit 10 s). Small = 1 disruption, medium = 2-3, large = 4-6 (sick pilots, delays of 1-5 h, cancellations). Methods: **cpsat** = CP-SAT minimum-change re-plan; **greedy** = greedy repair; **scratch** = CP-SAT re-solve from scratch (no change penalty).

Cells are mean (median). *Changes* = base-roster slots that got a different pilot; *uncrewed* = empty seats after the re-plan; *seconds* = solver time.

- CP-SAT re-plan needed **fewer changes than re-solving from scratch** in **50 of 50** scenarios (more in 0).
- CP-SAT re-plan left **fewer uncrewed seats than greedy repair** in **8 of 50** scenarios and more in 0 (the rest are ties).
- Total rule breaches found by the independent checker: cpsat 0, greedy 0, scratch 0.
- Uncrewed seats in total, of which on duties a delay made illegal (nobody may fly them): cpsat 25 (22), greedy 34 (22), scratch 25 (22).
- Re-solving from scratch hit its time limit (status FEASIBLE, not proven optimal) in 45/50 runs, so its numbers are of the best solution found in time.
- In the 8 scenarios where CP-SAT crewed more seats than greedy, greedy made fewer changes than CP-SAT in 8: it changes less partly because it leaves seats empty.
